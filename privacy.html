Privacy Policy

How we handle your details

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Who we are

ZUFA Living is a gated farm-estate community on Chevella Road, near Manneguda, Hyderabad. In this policy, "we" means the ZUFA Living team.

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry form on Facebook, Instagram or this website, we receive the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address and your answers about budget, plot size, purpose and buying timeline. We also receive basic information Meta passes on with the form, such as the time of submission.

How we use it

  • To call, message or email you with the plot list, price sheet and layout.
  • To arrange a private site visit.
  • To understand which questions people ask so we can improve our service.

We do not sell your details. We do not use them for anything unrelated to ZUFA Living.

Who can see it

Your details are visible to the ZUFA Living sales team and to the service providers who store them for us, such as Meta (which hosts the form) and Google (which holds our lead spreadsheet). They may process your details only to provide those services.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to follow up with you, and for up to 24 months after your last contact. After that we delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show, correct or delete your details, or to stop contacting you. Email zufaliving@gmail.com or call +91 98850 33844. We will act on your request promptly.
